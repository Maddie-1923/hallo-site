# Privacy Policy

Hallo is an app for tracking the shows and movies you watch. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, and it collects nothing about you.

## Everything you save stays on your phone

Your tracked shows and movies, what you've watched and when, your reactions and notes, your theme and accessibility settings, and your notification preferences are all stored on your device. None of it is sent anywhere, and none of it reaches me.

## iCloud sync is off

Hallo can sync your library through iCloud, and that feature is currently switched off and hidden. When it ships it will be opt-in, so nothing syncs unless you turn it on yourself.

When you do turn it on, your library goes to your own private iCloud database — the storage Apple gives you under your Apple Account. I have no access to it and no way to read what's in it. Posters and episode listings aren't synced; those come back from the sources below on their own.

## What Hallo sends to other services

To show you anything useful, Hallo asks three services for information about shows and movies:

- **TMDB** for show and movie details, posters, episode lists and cast
- **OMDb** for IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings
- **TVMaze** for episode air times

These requests carry the title or the ID of the thing being looked up, and nothing else. There is no account, no device identifier, and nothing about you attached to them. The services can see that someone asked about a particular show; they can't see who, and they aren't told anything about your library, your watch history or your settings.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy covering what they log about incoming requests.

## No analytics, no tracking, no ads

Hallo contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. There are no accounts to create and nothing to sign in to. Your data is never sold and never shared.

## Notifications

Notifications about new episodes, season premieres, movie releases and the weekly digest are scheduled entirely on your device. Hallo works out the dates from information it already has and hands iOS a list of reminders, which iOS then delivers on its own. There is no push server, and nothing about what you track leaves your phone in order to notify you.

## Poster images

Posters and stills are cached on your device so they load instantly and still appear when you're offline. The cache has a size limit and clears out the oldest images on its own. It lives in the system caches folder, which means iOS can also empty it whenever it needs the space, and it's removed completely when you delete the app.

## Your data is yours

You can export your entire library to a plain JSON file at any time from **Settings → Backup**. It's readable text, you can keep it wherever you like, and any copy of Hallo can take it back in.

You can delete everything by deleting the app. That removes your library, your watch history, your settings and the poster cache from your device. If iCloud sync is on, Settings also has a **Remove from iCloud** option that clears the copy stored there.

Because your data lives on your device rather than on a server I control, there's nothing for me to delete on your behalf and no request to send me.

## Children

Hallo doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
