# Support

Hallo keeps track of the shows and movies you're watching — what you've seen, what's waiting, and when the next episode airs. It runs entirely on your phone, with show details, ratings and air times pulled from TMDB, OMDb and TVMaze.

## Get in touch

**laudunlap@gmail.com**

I usually reply within two or three days.

## Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include your iOS version and what happened — what you tapped, what you expected, and what the app did instead. A screenshot is worth even more.

## About your data

Your library lives on your device, not on a server. Deleting the app deletes your tracked shows and movies, your watch history, your reactions and your notes along with it, and there's no copy for me to restore.

If you want to keep any of it, export first: **Settings → Backup → Export** writes everything to a JSON file you can save wherever you like, and any copy of Hallo can read it back in.
